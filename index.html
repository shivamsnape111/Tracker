Sacred Chants
🕉️ Om / Aum
ॐ  ॐ  ॐ
The primordial sound of the universe. Chant 108 times or for any duration. Calms the mind and aligns your energy before study.
🙏 Gayatri Mantra
ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्
Om Bhur Bhuva Svaha, Tat Savitur Varenyam, Bhargo Devasya Dhimahi, Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayat — "O Divine Light, illuminate our intellect." The most powerful mantra for students and seekers of knowledge.
🐘 Ganesh Mantra — Remove Obstacles
ॐ गं गणपतये नमः
Om Gam Ganapataye Namaha — Invoke Ganesha before any new beginning. Perfect before starting a study session or interview. Removes obstacles from your path to Google.
💛 Saraswati Mantra — Goddess of Knowledge
ॐ ऐं सरस्वत्यै नमः
Om Aim Saraswatyai Namaha — Seek the blessings of Saraswati for intelligence, memory, clarity of thought, and mastery of DSA & System Design.
🔱 Shiva Mantra — Strength & Focus
ॐ नमः शिवाय
Om Namah Shivaya — The five-syllable mantra of Lord Shiva. Chant for inner strength, discipline, and the willpower to stay consistent through the 6-month grind. 108 times is ideal.
🏆 Hanuman Mantra — Courage & Power
ॐ हं हनुमते नमः
Om Ham Hanumate Namaha — For unstoppable courage, confidence in interviews, and the energy to never give up. Chant when you feel low or anxious.
📿 Mahamrityunjaya — Protection & Healing
ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान् मृत्योर्मुक्षीय मामृतात्
Om Tryambakam Yajamahe... — For healing, protection, and release from fear. Chant on difficult days to restore calm and mental clarity.
Pranayama Breathing
🌬️ Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
Inhale 4 sec → Hold 4 sec → Exhale 4 sec → Hold 4 sec
Do 4-8 rounds before study or before an interview. Activates your parasympathetic system — calm, sharp, ready.
🧘 Anulom Vilom (Alternate Nostril)
Close right nostril → Inhale left 4 sec → Close left → Exhale right 4 sec → Repeat
5-10 minutes every morning. Balances both brain hemispheres — logical + creative. Perfect for DSA problem solving.
